Month over month

Image 1 · December 2025+296%

Monthly sales: AED 14,787 → AED 58,570

Gross sales were at AED 14,787 the month before. I grew them to AED 58,570 in December, a 296% increase. It was not a one-day spike: sales stayed above the previous month almost every day, and the month ended stronger than it started.

Week over week

Image 2 · One week, all platforms+75% orders

Orders up 75%, sales up 69% in one week

Weekly gross sales were down at around AED 10,700 from about 151 orders. A week later I had them at AED 18,131 from 264 orders. Net sales rose 31%, and the average order value held almost flat, so the growth came from more customers ordering, not bigger baskets.
